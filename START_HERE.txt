HOMECLOUD — START HERE

You do not need to write code.

MAC STEPS
1. Put this entire "HomeCloud" folder on your Desktop.
2. Open the HomeCloud folder.
3. Double-click: 01_SETUP_HOMECLOUD.command
4. macOS may ask for permission to open it. Allow it.
5. Wait for "SETUP COMPLETE".
6. Double-click: 02_RUN_HOMECLOUD.command
7. Keep that Terminal window open.
8. The Terminal will show:
   - Mac URL: http://127.0.0.1:5050
   - iPhone URL: http://YOUR-MAC-IP:5050
9. Put your iPhone on the SAME trusted Wi-Fi as the Mac.
10. Open Safari on the iPhone and type the iPhone URL.
11. Tap "Choose a photo", select one photo, then "Upload to my laptop".
12. On the Mac, open:
    Desktop > HomeCloud > HomeCloudStorage > photos
13. Your uploaded photo should be there.

IMPORTANT
- This first build is LOCAL ONLY.
- Do not port-forward 5050.
- Do not expose this prototype to the public internet.
- The random-looking stored filename is intentional: HomeCloud never trusts a phone-supplied filename as a disk filename.
